    Vision and Mission

    Moni Cloth was founded in 2018 by Pholile Mthlaba. Her inspiration for creating Moni Cloth was to offer elegance, luxury, and quality in clothes to women who did not have access to them due to financial constraints.

    Our Mission

    The mission of Moni Cloth is to offer women who are not in a good financial p to look good on a budget. We believe that everyone deserves to fee confident

    Our Vision

    The vision of the business is to boost the confidence of women and children globally. Through affordable fashion and sustainable practices, we aim to create a positive impact on our communities.
