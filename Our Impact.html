  • NGO Goal

    • As we move forward into the future, we are thankful and proud of our accomplishments and the generosity of our supporters.

    Global Reach: We have successfully reached a large percentage of women globally who needed assistance with additional clothes to wear during different seasons, especially winter. The women Moni Cloth frequently donated to and helped were mainly from African countries.

    Lives Changed: Since the year 2018 till 2026, Moni Cloth has assisted more than 5 million women and children in South Africa alone and about 8 million globally. Therefore, collectively Moni Cloth has assisted over 13 million women and children.

    Community Support: We have had 500,000 volunteers that helped Moni Cloth reach its NGO goal. There are many more volunteers that came through the collaboration of other NGOs and organizations committed to our mission.
