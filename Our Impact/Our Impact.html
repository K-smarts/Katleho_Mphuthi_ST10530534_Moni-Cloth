    • As we move forward into the future we are thankful and proud of our accomplishments and the generosity of our supporters.

    We have successfully reached a larget percentage of women globally,who needed assistance with additional clothes to wear during different seasons, especially winter.The women Moni Cloth frequently donated and helped were mainly from Africa countries.

    Since the year 2018 till 2026, Moni Cloth has assisted more that 5 million women and children in South Africa alone and about 8 million globally. Therefore collectively Moni Cloth has assisted over 13 million women and children.


    We have had 500 000 volunteers that helped Moni Cloth reach it's NGO goal. There are more volunteers that came through the collaboration of other NGO's.
